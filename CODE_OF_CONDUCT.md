# Code of conduct

`simtabi/vps-server-workbench` follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion about the work. Harassment
of any kind is not tolerated, in issues, pull requests, reviews or any other project space.

Report conduct concerns privately to [opensource@simtabi.com](mailto:opensource@simtabi.com). Reports are
handled confidentially by the maintainers, who may take any action the Covenant's enforcement guidelines
describe, from a private warning to a permanent ban.
